# Attack on Titan (Shingeki no Kyojin)

Manga by **Hajime Isayama** (2009–2021), anime by Wit Studio (Seasons 1–3) and MAPPA (Final Season). Humanity lives behind giant walls to hide from man-eating Titans… until the walls aren't safe anymore.

> ⚠️ **Spoiler guide:** sections are tagged by how far into the story they go.
> 🟢 = Season 1 safe · 🟡 = Seasons 2–3 · 🔴 = Final Season / ending

---

## 🟢 The world
- Humanity lives inside three walls: **Wall Maria** (outer), **Wall Rose** (middle) and **Wall Sina** (inner, where the rich live).
- **Titans** are giant humanoids that eat people. They can only be killed by slicing the **nape of the neck**.
- Soldiers fight with **ODM gear** (Omni-Directional Mobility gear): gas-powered grappling hooks and swords.

---

## 🟢 Military branches (factions inside the walls)
- **Survey Corps (Scout Regiment)** – go *outside* the walls to explore and fight. Highest death rate. Symbol: the Wings of Freedom.
- **Garrison** – guard and maintain the walls.
- **Military Police** – protect the king in Wall Sina; often corrupt.
- **Training Corps** – where cadets train before choosing a branch.

---

## 🟡 Bigger factions (later seasons)
- **Eldians** – the people who can turn into Titans. The people on Paradis Island (inside the walls) are Eldians.
- **Marley** – a nation across the sea that rules over the Eldians living there and uses Titans as weapons.
- **Warrior Unit** – Eldian children from Marley trained to inherit Titan powers.
- **Anti-Marleyan Volunteers** – led by Yelena; work with Paradis from the shadows. 🔴
- **Yeagerists** – Eren's followers who take over Paradis. 🔴

---

## 🟡 The Nine Titans
The nine special Titan powers passed down through the bloodline of Ymir.

| Titan | Power |
|---|---|
| **Founding** | Controls other Titans and can alter Eldian memories |
| **Attack** | Always fights for freedom; sees future memories 🔴 |
| **Colossal** | 60 m tall; releases a massive explosion of heat and steam |
| **Armored** | Covered in hardened armor plates |
| **Female** | Agile; can call regular Titans with a scream |
| **Beast** | Throws objects with deadly accuracy |
| **Jaw** | Small, fast, with extremely strong jaws and claws |
| **Cart** | Built for endurance; can carry weapons |
| **War Hammer** | Creates weapons and structures out of hardened material |

**The curse of Ymir:** anyone who inherits one of the nine Titans only lives about **13 more years**.

## Key events (in order)

### 🟢 Season 1
1. **Fall of Wall Maria (year 845)** – the Colossal and Armored Titans break through the outer wall. Eren's mother is eaten.
2. **Battle of Trost** – Eren discovers he can turn into a Titan and helps seal a hole in Wall Rose.
3. **57th Expedition** – the Survey Corps is hunted by the intelligent **Female Titan**.
4. **Female Titan revealed** – it's Annie. She seals herself in crystal.

### 🟡 Seasons 2–3
5. **Titans found inside the walls** – the walls themselves are built from Colossal Titans.
6. **Reiner and Bertholdt revealed** as the Armored and Colossal Titans.
7. **Uprising arc** – the Survey Corps overthrows the fake king; Historia becomes queen.
8. **Return to Shiganshina** – Wall Maria is retaken. Erwin dies leading a suicide charge; Armin inherits the Colossal Titan.
9. **The basement** – Grisha Yeager's notes reveal the truth: there is a whole world beyond the sea, and Paradis is its enemy.

### 🔴 Final Season
10. **Marley arc** – the story switches to the "enemy" side and the children of the Warrior Unit, such as Gabi and Falco.
11. **Raid on Liberio** – Eren attacks Marley during a festival declaring war on Paradis. Sasha is killed by Gabi.
12. **Eren's betrayal** – Eren breaks away from his friends and sides with Zeke (the Beast Titan) to reach the Founding Titan's power.
13. **The Rumbling** – Eren unleashes millions of Colossal Titans from the walls to destroy the world outside Paradis.
14. **Alliance** – former enemies (Survey Corps survivors and Marley's warriors) team up to stop Eren. Hange sacrifices herself.
15. **Battle of Heaven and Earth** – the final fight. Mikasa kills Eren, ending the Rumbling.
16. **Ending** – Titan powers vanish from the world. Armin becomes an ambassador for peace. The cycle of hatred is left as an open question.

## Big themes
- **Freedom vs. safety** – is living behind walls really living?
- **Cycle of hatred** – both sides think they're the heroes.
- **No clear good guys** – the story keeps flipping who you sympathise with.
- **Sacrifice** – almost every victory costs many lives.
