# Red Hot Chili Peppers

Funk-rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1983. Inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2012. Known for mixing funk bass, punk energy, melodic rock and rap-style vocals.

---

## Band members

### Current lineup
| Member | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Anthony Kiedis** | Lead vocals | Founding member; writes most of the lyrics |
| **Flea** (Michael Balzary) | Bass, trumpet | Founding member; slap bass is the band's signature sound |
| **Chad Smith** | Drums | Joined 1988; tight, heavy groove |
| **John Frusciante** | Guitar, backing vocals | Joined 1988, left 1992, back 1998–2009, back again since 2019 |

### Notable former members
- **Hillel Slovak** – original guitarist (died 1988). His funky style shaped the early sound.
- **Jack Irons** – original drummer.
- **Dave Navarro** – guitar on *One Hot Minute* (1995), heavier and darker.
- **Josh Klinghoffer** – guitar 2009–2019 (*I'm with You*, *The Getaway*).
- Others along the way: Jack Sherman, Cliff Martinez, DeWayne McKnight, D.H. Peligro, Arik Marshall, Jesse Tobias.

## Favourite songs and why

1. **Under the Bridge** (*Blood Sugar Sex Magik*) – The clean intro is one of the most recognisable guitar parts in rock. It shows Frusciante's Hendrix-style chord embellishments, and the song is honest and vulnerable in a way the band's early party songs weren't.
2. **Can't Stop** (*By the Way*) – The muted, choppy guitar riff is a masterclass in rhythm playing: what you *don't* play matters as much as what you do. Great for practising palm muting and right-hand control.
3. **Snow (Hey Oh)** (*Stadium Arcadium*) – A fast, flowing single-note pattern that runs through the whole song. It's a stamina and accuracy workout and sounds full even though it's simple.
4. **Scar Tissue** (*Californication*) – Proof that less is more. Mostly sparse double-stops (two notes at once) and a slide-guitar solo. Very tasteful.
5. **Give It Away** (*Blood Sugar Sex Magik*) – Pure funk energy. Flea's bass line drives everything, and it's the clearest example of the band's original sound.
6. **Californication** (title track) – A simple, moody arpeggio with one of their best melodies. Easy to start learning, hard to play with the right feel.
7. **Dani California** (*Stadium Arcadium*) – Big classic-rock chorus with a fiery outro solo; ties together characters from older songs.
8. **Otherside** (*Californication*) – Haunting, minor-key and catchy; a good one for learning how a simple riff can carry a whole song.

## Fun facts
- Frusciante joined the band at 18 as a huge fan of Hillel Slovak.
- *Blood Sugar Sex Magik* was recorded in a mansion the band reportedly believed was haunted.
- Flea also played bass for other projects and acted in films.
