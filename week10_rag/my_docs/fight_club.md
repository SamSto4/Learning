# Fight Club

- **Book:** novel by **Chuck Palahniuk** (1996)
- **Movie:** directed by **David Fincher** (1999), starring **Edward Norton** (the Narrator), **Brad Pitt** (Tyler Durden) and **Helena Bonham Carter** (Marla Singer)
- Flopped at the box office, then became a huge cult classic on DVD.

> ⚠️ The **Plot twist** section has the big spoiler. Skip it if you haven't watched the movie yet.

---

## Main characters
| Character | Who they are |
|---|---|
| **The Narrator** | Unnamed office worker with insomnia, stuck in a boring, consumer-driven life |
| **Tyler Durden** | Charismatic, chaotic soap salesman who rejects everything the Narrator lives for |
| **Marla Singer** | Self-destructive woman the Narrator keeps running into at support groups |
| **Robert "Bob" Paulson** | Former bodybuilder the Narrator meets at a support group; becomes a Project Mayhem member |
| **Angel Face** | Fight Club member (played by Jared Leto in the movie) |

---

## Plot points (spoiler-light)
1. **Insomnia** – the Narrator can't sleep and fills his empty life buying IKEA-style furniture.
2. **Support groups** – he starts going to support groups for illnesses he doesn't have. Crying there finally lets him sleep.
3. **Marla** – another "tourist" shows up at the groups and ruins the escape for him.
4. **Meeting Tyler** – on a plane, the Narrator meets Tyler Durden.
5. **The apartment explodes** – he has nowhere to go and calls Tyler.
6. **The first fight** – outside a bar, Tyler asks the Narrator to hit him. They both feel more alive.
7. **Fight Club forms** – other men join and the fights move to a bar basement.
8. **Project Mayhem** – Tyler turns the club into an anti-consumerist army that carries out acts of sabotage.
9. **Things spiral** – the Narrator realises he has lost control of what Tyler is building.

---

## The plot twist 🔴
- **Tyler isn't real.** He is the Narrator's alter ego: a split personality that comes out when the Narrator "sleeps."
- Everything Tyler did, the Narrator did himself, including blowing up his own apartment.
- Project Mayhem plans to blow up credit-card company buildings to wipe out debt records.
- In the end, the Narrator shoots himself through the cheek to "kill" Tyler, and watches the buildings collapse while holding Marla's hand.
- **Book vs. movie:** in the book the bombs fail, and the Narrator wakes up in a mental hospital that he believes is heaven. Some staff there are Project Mayhem members.

## The rules (paraphrased)
The famous first rule: **"You do not talk about Fight Club."** The rest, summed up:
1. Don't talk about Fight Club.
2. Seriously, *don't* talk about Fight Club (the rule is repeated for emphasis).
3. A fight ends when someone taps out, says stop or goes limp.
4. Only two people fight at a time.
5. One fight at a time.
6. No shirts and no shoes.
7. Fights go on as long as they have to.
8. If it's your first night, you have to fight.

## Famous lines and ideas (described, not quoted in full)
- Tyler's speech about how **the things you own end up owning you**.
- The idea that people work jobs they hate to buy things they don't need.
- Tyler calling the members a **"middle children of history"** generation with no great war or great purpose.
- The speech about how **only after you lose everything are you free to do anything**.
- The Narrator naming himself after organs from a magazine article ("I am Jack's…").

---

## Themes
- **Consumerism** – finding identity through products and brands.
- **Masculinity in crisis** – men feeling purposeless in modern life.
- **Identity and the self** – the split between who you are and who you want to be.
- **Control and anarchy** – a rebellion against control turns into a cult with even stricter rules.
- **Satire, not a guide** – Fincher and Palahniuk present Tyler as seductive *and* dangerous. Many viewers miss that the film criticises him.
